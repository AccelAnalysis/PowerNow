## Editorial design principles

- **Clarity creates speed:** planning is used to move decisions upstream so execution contains fewer unnecessary questions; the manuscript does not claim that more planning always means faster results.
- **Evidence without academic drag:** major claims are supported in the narrative, with full study notes collected in back matter.
- **The book is the workspace:** when a chapter asks the reader to define, sort, map, prioritize, or decide something, the manuscript provides space to complete that action directly in the physical book rather than sending the reader to a separate worksheet.
- **Current series architecture:** Book 1 Chapter 3 is *The NOW Success Cycle*. Material on guarding execution capacity belongs to Book 4 and should not be duplicated here.
